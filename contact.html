Cloud Engineering & Advisory FAQs

Everything you need to know about engaging with ZeroBytech's architecture team.

Our architecture review is a deep-dive technical examination conducted by senior systems architects. We inspect your existing infrastructure topology, Infrastructure as Code repositories, container configurations, and CI/CD pipelines. We evaluate five core pillars: Reliability, Performance, Security Posture, Operational Automation, and FinOps Cost Optimization. Within 5 business days, you receive a written report with an actionable risk matrix, specific remediation steps, and an estimate of potential cloud savings.

Migration timelines depend on workload complexity and database sizes, but typically follow our 4-phase framework spanning 4 to 12 weeks. We first audit and codify the target landing zone (Weeks 1-3), establish live bi-directional data replication bridges (Weeks 3-6), run staging workloads in parallel with production (Weeks 6-8), and execute a zero-downtime cutover during low-traffic windows. Every migration phase includes pre-verified rollback mechanisms so your business never risks prolonged disruption.

We do not operate as an isolated black-box agency that hands over an unmaintainable codebase. ZeroBytech pairs directly with your internal engineers through structured architecture walkthroughs, pair-programming sessions, and exhaustive documentation. We build intuitive GitOps workflows and self-healing systems so that your internal team feels completely confident maintaining, extending, and operating their new cloud platform from day one.

We have deep production expertise across Amazon Web Services (AWS), Google Cloud Platform (GCP), and Microsoft Azure. We also frequently architect hybrid-cloud and colocation migration topologies using Terraform, Pulumi, Kubernetes (EKS, GKE, AKS, and bare-metal Talos/k3s), and encrypted VPN/DirectConnect interconnects. We remain strictly cloud-neutral and recommend the specific provider and services that best match your workload throughput, compliance requirements, and budget constraints.

We engineer the resilient technical foundation required for modern enterprise scale. High-throughput cloud infrastructure, codified and automated.
